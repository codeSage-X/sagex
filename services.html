01 · Software development

The full lifecycle

Six stages, start to finish. Come in at the beginning, or hand us something half-built and we'll pick it up from wherever it stalled.

STAGE 01

Discovery & scoping

We work out what's actually being built and what it costs before anyone opens an editor. You get scope, timeline, and risk in plain sentences you can hold us to.

STAGE 02

Product design

Flows, screens, and a component setdesigned against real content, not lorem ipsum, so the build has nothing left to guess at.

STAGE 03

Build

Short cycles with something running at the end of each one. Web platforms, mobile apps, and the APIs and databases underneath them.

STAGE 04

QA & launch

Real-device testing, load and edge cases, deploy pipeline, monitoring and alerts wired up before the first user arrives.

STAGE 05

Handover

Source, infrastructure, and accounts in your name, with documentation and a walkthrough for whoever maintains it next including us, if that's the arrangement.

STAGE 06

Care & continuity

Retained support after launch: fixes, dependency and security updates, performance work, and new features as the product earns them.
